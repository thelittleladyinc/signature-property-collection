The Concierge Experience

Estate homes, acreage, and architecturally significant properties deserve a process built for them — not a generic transaction. Here's what that actually looks like.

What Sets It Apart

Three Things Every Client Gets

Private Showings & Off-Market Access

A focused, private search — including off-market and pre-public inventory — instead of the same public listings every buyer sees on Zillow.

Luxury Marketing & Staging

Cinematic video, drone footage, and print campaigns built for $1M+ listings, paired with professional staging so the home photographs the way it actually lives.

One Point Of Contact

Relocation and out-of-state logistics, trusted local vendors, and a single person — not a rotating cast — from first call to closing.

The Process

What Working Together Looks Like

01 · Discovery

A real conversation about what you're looking for (or what your home is worth) — no cookie-cutter questionnaire.

02 · Strategy

Pricing, positioning, and marketing built around your specific property or search, informed by Certified Negotiation Specialist and Luxury Home Marketing Expert training.

03 · Showings & Offers

Private tours or showings, and a well-crafted offer or listing strategy — including VA loan expertise for veteran buyers.

04 · Negotiation

Earnest money, inspection, and negotiation handled directly — not handed off.

05 · Closing

Radon testing, final walkthrough, and a coordinated path to closing day.

06 · After The Close

The relationship doesn't end at closing — ask any of the clients in our reviews.
