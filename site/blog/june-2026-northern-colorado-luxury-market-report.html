What This Means For Luxury Owners

The $1M+ tier here is a different conversation than the median market. Days on market run longer because buyers are deliberate, not hesitant. Sale-to-list ratios tighten because both sides arrive prepared. And pricing precision — not pricing volume — determines whether your property trades within 60 days or sits for six months.

Whether you are evaluating a gated estate in Windsor, a waterfront retreat on Boyd Lake, or an architect-designed home in Boulder County, the strategy starts with understanding which segment you are actually in. Average data won’t get you there.
