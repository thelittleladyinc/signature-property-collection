Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Northern Colorado real estate market doing right now?

As of June 2026, the weighted median sale price across Northern Colorado is $575,000, homes are averaging 56 days on market, and sellers are averaging 98.3% of list. Sellers are still landing close to ask, and buyers are winning on the homes that were priced properly to begin with. In the $1M+ tier days on market run longer because those buyers are deliberate rather than hesitant -- pricing precision, not pricing volume, decides whether a luxury property trades inside 60 days or sits for six months. Figures from IRES MLS, trailing 60 days.

Is it a good time to sell a home in Northern Colorado?

On the current numbers, a well-priced home is still selling close to ask — the average sale-to-list ratio is 98.3%. The word doing the work there is well-priced. Homes that come out over the market sit, go stale, and then sell for less than they would have. That is why pricing is the first of the three questions worth asking any agent.

How is the luxury market different from the median market here?

Days on market run longer at the top — 47.5 days median for the 18 homes that closed above $1M — and that is deliberation rather than weakness. Sale-to-list tightens because both sides arrive prepared. Averages across the whole market will not tell you anything useful about a $1.5M property; you need the figures for your actual segment.

Where do these numbers come from?

IRES MLS — the same multiple listing service used to price every listing, over the trailing 60 days. Not a Zestimate and not a national aggregator's model. Aggregate statistics only; individual addresses are held privately.
