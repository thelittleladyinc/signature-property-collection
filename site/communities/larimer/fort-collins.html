Fort Collins

Welcome To Fort Collins

Fort Collins is a vibrant and welcoming city in Northern Colorado, nestled at the foothills of the Rocky Mountains. Known for its thriving craft beer scene, top-rated schools, and abundant outdoor recreation, it offers the perfect balance of small-town charm and big-city amenities. The city features tree-lined streets with historic homes, modern new-build communities, and stunning luxury properties. Neighborhoods like Old Town radiate character and charm, while areas such as Harmony Corridor provide spacious homes with all the conveniences. Whether you’re drawn by its entrepreneurial spirit, world-class biking trails, or vibrant arts scene, Fort Collins is a place where every lifestyle feels at home.

Things To Do In Fort Collins

Experience the charm of Old Town Fort Collins, with its preserved 19th-century architecture, unique boutiques, and vibrant dining scene. Old Town is the subject of local lore that its Victorian-era storefronts helped inspire Disneyland's Main Street, U.S.A. — Disney artist Harper Goff, who worked on Main Street's design, grew up in Fort Collins and reportedly showed Walt Disney photos of his hometown, though Disney's most-cited inspiration remains his own hometown of Marceline, Missouri. Engage with interactive exhibits that blend science and history at the Fort Collins Museum of Discovery. Just west of the city, Horsetooth Reservoir offers boating, swimming, fishing, and hiking against stunning mountain vistas. Stroll through the Gardens on Spring Creek, a 12-acre botanical oasis featuring themed gardens and a butterfly house. Enjoy outdoor adventures along the Cache la Poudre River — Colorado's only federally designated National Wild and Scenic River — ideal for whitewater rafting, fishing, and scenic hikes. Tour the New Belgium Brewing Company, one of the nation's largest craft breweries, still headquartered in Fort Collins. Relive the 1980s at Totally 80's Pizza & Museum, where you can enjoy a slice amidst a vast collection of 80s memorabilia.

Restaurants & Dining

Old Town's dining scene blends comfort food with global flavors. Lucile's Restaurant is a Fort Collins institution for New Orleans-style breakfast, while The Silver Grill Cafe — open since 1933 — is beloved for its towering cinnamon rolls. CooperSmith's Brewpub, right on Old Town Square, is one of the city's original craft breweries and a great people-watching spot.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Fort Collins has several city-run off-leash dog parks. Spring Canyon Community Park (2626 W. Horsetooth Rd.) has the largest, most amenity-rich dog park at the base of the foothills, with tunnels and a doggy pond. Fossil Creek Park and Twin Silo Community Park both offer smaller fenced dog areas in south and southeast Fort Collins.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Fort Collins

Horsetooth Mountain Open Space, just west of the city, offers the iconic climb to Horsetooth Rock plus the seasonal Horsetooth Falls, with 29 miles of connected trails leading into Lory State Park. Closer to town, the paved Poudre River Trail follows the Cache la Poudre through Fort Collins for an easy walk or bike ride.

Schools & Commute From Fort Collins

Schools: Poudre School District R-1. Commute: About 64 miles / roughly 1 hour 17 minutes to Denver via I-25 (longer with typical traffic); about 20 miles / 37 minutes to Berthoud, and roughly 10 miles to Timnath.
