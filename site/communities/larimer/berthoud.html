Berthoud

Welcome To Berthoud

Berthoud, Colorado, is a picturesque town often called the “Garden Spot of Colorado” for its lush landscapes and tree-lined streets. Nestled between Longmont and Loveland, it combines small-town charm with stunning views of the Rocky Mountains. Known for its tight-knit community, Berthoud offers a welcoming atmosphere, historic character, and a love for outdoor living, making it a truly special place to call home.

What It's Like To Live In Berthoud

Things To Do In Berthoud

Visit City Star Brewing to enjoy handcrafted beers in a welcoming atmosphere. The brewery often hosts live music and community events, making it a local favorite. Explore Carter Lake Reservoir, a popular spot for boating, fishing, camping, and hiking. The surrounding trails offer scenic views and opportunities for wildlife observation. Delve into local history at the Little Thompson Valley Pioneer Museum, which showcases artifacts and exhibits detailing Berthoud's rich heritage. Experience wine tasting at Blue Mountain Vineyards, where you can sample a variety of locally produced wines in a picturesque setting. For astronomy enthusiasts, the Little Thompson Observatory offers public viewing nights, providing a chance to explore the night sky through powerful telescopes.

Restaurants & Dining

Berthoud's Main Street has a growing food scene anchored by cozy local spots. Joyful Brews is a beloved coffee shop just off Main Street, while Trailhead Cafe and Cornerstone Cafe are popular for hearty breakfasts and lunch. For dinner, Berthoud Pizza Company is a longtime local favorite for pizza and fresh salads.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

The town operates the Berthoud Dog Park at 202 10th St., a fully fenced, double-gated off-leash area. Carter Lake Reservoir, just west of town, also allows leashed dogs on its trails and shorelines.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Berthoud

The Carter Lake Reservoir Sundance Trail is the standout — a scenic, mostly flat, roughly 6-mile out-and-back along the lake's west shore (a park entrance fee applies). For a shorter outing close to town, Pinewood Reservoir near Ramsay-Shockey Open Space offers additional lake-view hiking a short drive from Berthoud.

Schools & Commute From Berthoud

Schools: Thompson School District R2-J. Commute: About 7 miles (12 min) to Loveland, 20 miles (37 min) to Fort Collins via US-287, and roughly 46 miles (about 56 min) to Denver via I-25. Berthoud is one of the faster-growing towns in Larimer County, with active master-planned new-home communities such as Berthoud Harvest and Farmstead adding significant new-construction inventory to a historically small-town market.
