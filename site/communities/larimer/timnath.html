Timnath

Welcome To Timnath

Timnath, Colorado, is a peaceful and rapidly growing town that perfectly balances small-town charm with modern living. Located just east of Fort Collins, it offers beautiful open spaces, family-friendly neighborhoods, and a strong sense of community. With its stunning views of the Rockies and easy access to outdoor activities, Timnath is an idyllic place to call home.

Life In Timnath

What It's Like To Live In Timnath

Things To Do In Timnath

Enjoy fishing, boating, and bird-watching at Timnath Reservoir, a popular spot for outdoor enthusiasts. Visit Timnath Beerwerks to sample a variety of craft beers, with regular food-truck visits and live music. Stroll through Historic Old Town Timnath, featuring historic buildings, local shops, and eateries. Nearby, the Arapaho Bend Natural Area — a City of Fort Collins natural area just west of Timnath — offers trails for hiking and wildlife viewing.

Restaurants & Dining

The Farmhouse at Jessup Farm offers farm-to-table dining in a restored historic farmhouse at Jessup Farm Artisan Village. Timnath Beerwerks pairs rotating craft beer with regular food-truck visits, and Doug's Diner is a longtime local breakfast-and-lunch spot.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Timnath has its own dedicated off-leash Timnath Dog Park, and the newer WildWing Park (opened 2025) also includes a designated dog park area — leashes are required elsewhere in town parks.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Timnath

The paved Poudre River Trail runs directly through Timnath, connecting to Fort Collins and Greeley. Arapaho Bend Natural Area, just west near the Timnath/Fort Collins line, has an easy loop with ponds and prairie-dog colonies.

Schools & Commute From Timnath

Schools: Poudre School District R-1. Commute: About 10 miles (17 min) to Fort Collins via CO-14, and about 57 miles (roughly 1 hour 7 minutes) to Denver via I-25. Timnath's rapid growth is almost entirely master-planned-community development (WildWing, Timnath Ranch), and most of these communities layer both an HOA and a metropolitan district on top of base property taxes — a real budgeting factor for relocating buyers.
