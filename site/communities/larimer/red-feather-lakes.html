Red Feather Lakes

Welcome To Red Feather Lakes

Red Feather Lakes, Colorado, is a serene mountain village offering a peaceful retreat surrounded by breathtaking natural beauty. Tucked away in the Roosevelt National Forest, it’s a haven for outdoor enthusiasts with endless opportunities for hiking, fishing, and wildlife watching. Known for its tranquil lakes and rustic charm, Red Feather Lakes is the perfect escape for those seeking a quiet, nature-focused lifestyle.

Life In Red Feather Lakes

What It's Like To Live In Red Feather Lakes

Things To Do In Red Feather Lakes

Formerly known as Shambhala Mountain Center, the Drala Mountain Center is a renowned Buddhist retreat home to the Great Stupa of Dharmakaya, widely described as the largest stupa in North America. Enjoy outdoor activities at Dowdy Lake, a 115-acre reservoir ideal for kayaking, canoeing, and fishing, with a 2.3-mile trail looping the water. Experience the whimsical charm of Gnome Road, a dirt path lined with ceramic gnomes and fairy doors. For panoramic views, visit Deadman Lookout, a historic fire tower. Golf enthusiasts can play a round at The Golf Club at Fox Acres, an 18-hole course open to the public since 2014.

Restaurants & Dining

Dining options are intentionally low-key given the setting. The Tavern at Red Feather Lakes is the classic local spot for a burger and a cold beer, and Potbelly Restaurant is known for homemade pies and hearty comfort food. A bit further out toward Livermore, the historic Forks Mercantity and Saloon is a well-known roadside landmark for a meal on the way in or out.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Red Feather Lakes itself has no formal dog park — it's rural mountain terrain where dogs typically go leashed or under voice control on trails and forest roads within the surrounding Roosevelt National Forest. For a true fenced off-leash dog park, the nearest option is in Fort Collins, about 45 miles (roughly 70 minutes) away.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Red Feather Lakes

The Dowdy Lake Trail (2.3-mile loop) circles the largest lake in the Red Feather Lakes chain. The Lady Moon and Molly Moon Trail (roughly 6.5 miles round trip) is a favorite for a longer forest hike with moderate elevation gain.

Schools & Commute From Red Feather Lakes

Schools: Poudre School District R-1 (local schooling via PSD's Mountain Schools cluster, including Red Feather Lakes Elementary; older students bus toward Fort Collins/Laporte schools). Commute: About 45 miles / roughly 1 hour 10 minutes to Fort Collins via CR 74E — the primary commute route and nearest job center. Real estate here is dominated by well-and-septic, large-acreage mountain and cabin lots (often 1+ acres, many seasonal or off-grid) — a distinctly different buyer profile than the platted town lots common down on the plains.
