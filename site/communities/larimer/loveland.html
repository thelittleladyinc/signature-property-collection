Welcome To Loveland

Loveland, Colorado, is a charming city celebrated for its artistic vibe, scenic beauty, and warm community spirit. Nestled near the Rocky Mountains, it offers stunning landscapes, a rich arts scene, and plenty of opportunities for outdoor adventures. Known as the “Sweetheart City,” Loveland blends creativity and nature seamlessly, making it a unique and inviting place to live.

Life In Loveland

What It's Like To Live In Loveland

Things To Do In Loveland

Explore the Benson Park Sculpture Garden, featuring over 150 unique sculptures set amidst beautifully landscaped grounds. It's a perfect spot for art enthusiasts and families alike. Devil's Backbone Open Space Hike the Devil's Backbone Nature Trail, known for its distinctive rock formations and panoramic views. This area offers trails suitable for various skill levels. Enjoy a day of boating, fishing, or swimming at Boyd Lake State Park. The park also provides picnic areas and trails for biking and hiking. Delve into local history and art at the Loveland Museum, which offers a range of exhibits, educational programs, and events throughout the year. Visit the Chapungu Sculpture Park, a 26-acre park showcasing stone sculptures from Zimbabwean artists, complemented by walking paths and water features. Catch a live performance or film at the historic Rialto Theater Center, a beautifully restored venue that hosts a variety of cultural events.

Restaurants & Dining

Loveland's dining scene ranges from award-winning barbecue to craft beer. Nordy's Bar-B-Que & Grill is a longtime local favorite for smoked meats, while Door 222 Food & Drink covers upscale steak-and-seafood nights out. For casual sips, Verboten Brewing and Loveland Aleworks are both well-loved, independently owned local breweries with regularly rotating taps.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Loveland's main off-leash area is the Dog Park at Fairgrounds Park (700 S. Railroad Ave), a city-run facility with separate fenced sections for large and small dogs and a water fountain. Many of the city's natural areas, including trails along the Big Thompson River, are leashed-dog-friendly as well.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Loveland

Devil's Backbone Open Space remains the signature hike, with roughly 12 miles of trail leading to the landmark rock formation known as the Keyhole. For an easier outing, the Round Mountain Foothills Nature Trail is a gentle one-mile loop, and Prairie Ridge Natural Area offers a 2.4-mile multi-use trail system in northwest Loveland.

Schools & Commute From Loveland

Schools: Thompson School District R2-J. Commute: About 51 miles / roughly 1 hour 4 minutes to Denver via I-25; about 33 miles / roughly 49 minutes to Boulder via CO-119. Loveland hosts Sculpture in the Park each August at Benson Park, one of the largest outdoor fine-art sculpture shows in the country — a signature annual event tied to the city's arts-town identity.
