Broomfield

Welcome To Broomfield

Broomfield, Colorado, is a vibrant city offering a perfect mix of small-town charm and modern amenities. Nestled between Denver and Boulder, it boasts stunning open spaces, top-rated schools, and a strong sense of community. With its scenic parks, recreational opportunities, and thriving local economy, Broomfield is an exceptional place to live, work, and play.

Life In Broomfield

What It's Like To Live In Broomfield

Things To Do In Broomfield

Experience premier shopping at FlatIron Crossing Mall, featuring retail stores, dining options, and the adjacent 14-screen AMC Theatres Flatiron Crossing cinema. Delve into local history at the Broomfield Veterans Memorial Museum. Enjoy outdoor activities at Broomfield County Commons Park, a 300-acre space featuring sports fields, playgrounds, and walking trails. Cool off during the summer months at The Bay Aquatic Park, a family-friendly water park with pools and water slides.

Restaurants & Dining

Broomfield's dining scene punches above its size, especially around the Arista district. VINCA at Arista offers upscale European-inspired fine dining, while Hickory & Ash nearby serves a Colorado-centric steakhouse menu. For something more casual, The Burns Pub & Restaurant is a longtime family-owned British pub known for shepherd's pie and live music.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Broomfield County Commons Dog Park, inside the 300-acre County Commons complex, is fully fenced with separate areas and agility obstacles for dogs of all sizes.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Broomfield

The Coal Creek Trail is a 14-mile soft-surface path connecting Broomfield to Superior and Louisville. The Carolyn Holmberg Preserve at Rock Creek Farm offers a quieter network of shorter trails around Stearns Lake, good for bald-eagle spotting, and the Lake Link Trail, an 8.8-mile route, connects Broomfield's eastern and western trail systems.

Schools & Commute From Broomfield

Schools: Adams 12 Five Star Schools (primary district for most of the city; a portion of northwest Broomfield falls in Boulder Valley School District Re-2). Commute: About 42 miles / roughly 45-50 minutes to Loveland, largely via US-287/I-25. Baseline, a large master-planned community between Denver and Boulder, is actively selling new-construction homes from the $500s to $1M+, with a walkable retail district and a new park slated to open in 2027.
