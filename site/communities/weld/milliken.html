Milliken

Welcome To Milliken

Milliken, Colorado, is a peaceful and welcoming town known for its close-knit community and small-town charm. Situated along the South Platte River, it offers scenic beauty, a relaxed pace of life, and easy access to nearby cities like Greeley and Loveland. With its friendly atmosphere and emphasis on outdoor living, Milliken is an ideal place to enjoy Colorado’s natural beauty and community spirit.

What It's Like To Live In Milliken

Things To Do In Milliken

Built in 1911, the Daniels School is the last standing rural brick schoolhouse in Weld County and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. It operated for nearly 50 years (1911-1959) and now serves as a historical landmark. Ehrlich Lake and Centennial Lake provide short walking trails and are stocked with fish by Colorado Parks & Wildlife, making them perfect spots for fishing and leisurely strolls. For golf enthusiasts, The Mad Russian Golf Club offers a scenic 18-hole course. Skateboarders can visit the Milliken Skatepark.

Restaurants & Dining

Mill Haüs Coffee is a local coffee shop and bakery serving paninis and pastries — a real neighborhood gathering spot. For a casual meal, El Mex-Kal Restaurant offers dine-in and takeout Mexican food.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Milliken has its own dedicated off-leash area: the Milliken Dog Park, the only spot in town where dogs are permitted off-leash — leashes are required everywhere else in town parks.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Milliken

Ehrlich Lake and Centennial Lake both have short, easy walking trails looping the water. The South Platte River, which runs along town, also offers informal walking and fishing access.

Schools & Commute From Milliken

Schools: Weld RE-5J (Johnstown-Milliken School District). Commute: About 14 miles / 27 minutes to Greeley via US-34 Business; about 15 miles / 27 minutes to Loveland via CO-60. Milliken sits within Weld County's high-growth corridor — the county was Colorado's fastest-growing large county in 2025, adding nearly 50,000 residents since 2020.
