Erie

Welcome To Erie

Erie, Colorado, is a vibrant town that blends small-town charm with modern amenities and a lively community spirit. Nestled between Boulder and Denver, Erie offers stunning mountain views, beautiful parks, and top-rated schools. With its thriving downtown, outdoor recreation opportunities, and welcoming atmosphere, Erie provides an exceptional quality of life in Northern Colorado.

Life In Erie

What It's Like To Live In Erie

Things To Do In Erie

Visit the Luvin Arms Animal Sanctuary to meet rescued farm animals through guided tours. Experience seasonal fun at Anderson Farms, known for its 25-acre corn maze, pumpkin patch, and hayrides during the fall season. Enjoy a leisurely walk or fishing at Thomas Reservoir. Spend time at Erie Community Park, a roughly 40-acre space featuring picnic shelters, sports fields, a skatepark, and walking trails. Explore Erie's history at the Wise Homestead Museum, a late 19th-century homestead.

Restaurants & Dining

24 Carrot Bistro is a local favorite for weekend brunch, known for its eggs Benedict. The Old Mine is the go-to for comfort food — great pizza and barbecue — and Echo Brewing and Pizzeria rounds things out with house-brewed beer and wood-fired pizza.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Erie has its own dedicated off-leash park: The Boneyard at Reliance Park, a 7.9-acre facility with a double-gated entry and separate fenced areas for large and small dogs.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Erie

The Erie Forest Trail is a beginner-friendly connector trail through Erie's growing singletrack network. The Coal Creek Trail runs along Erie's edge toward Broomfield and Louisville, and Thomas Reservoir has its own walking loop around the water.

Schools & Commute From Erie

Schools: St. Vrain Valley Schools serves most of Erie (including Erie High School); a portion of town falls in Boulder Valley School District (BVSD) depending on the neighborhood. Commute: About 27 miles / 39 minutes to Denver via I-25, and roughly 15-18 miles to Boulder — within a reasonable daily commute of both job centers. Erie is one of the southern Weld County towns seeing outsized growth from buyers priced out of Boulder County and metro Denver.
