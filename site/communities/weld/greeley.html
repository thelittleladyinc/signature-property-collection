Welcome To Greeley

Greeley, Colorado, is a vibrant city with a rich history and a thriving cultural scene. As a hub of Northern Colorado, it combines urban amenities with a welcoming, small-town feel. Known for its strong agricultural roots, excellent schools, and diverse recreational opportunities, Greeley offers a dynamic lifestyle surrounded by the natural beauty of the region.

Things To Do In Greeley

Explore an extensive collection of model trains and intricate layouts at the Colorado Model Railroad Museum. Enjoy a scenic walk or bike ride along the Poudre River Trail, a 21-mile paved path with access to the Cache la Poudre River for fishing and wildlife observation. Step back in time at the Centennial Village Museum, an 8-acre living history experience showcasing over 35 unique structures, costumed interpreters, and heritage farm animals. Attend a performance at the Union Colony Civic Center, one of Northern Colorado's premier performing arts venues, hosting Broadway musicals, concerts, and local productions. Experience the annual Greeley Independence Stampede, a PRCA-sanctioned rodeo and summer festival featuring concerts, a demolition derby, and a Fourth of July parade.

Restaurants & Dining

Greeley's dining scene mixes destination craft beer with longtime local favorites. WeldWerks Brewing Co. is an award-winning brewery known well beyond Weld County for its burgers and rotating small-batch beers. For a sit-down Italian dinner, locals point to Pellegrini Cucina Italiana, while Roasty's Diner, a downtown Greeley institution since the early 1970s (under new ownership since 2022, still serving its old-school breakfast and lunch menu plus a new Greek bakery case), is a local go-to.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Greeley has two dedicated off-leash dog parks: Island Grove Dog Park (14th Ave & D St), with separate small- and large-dog areas, and Rover Run Dog Park (near 5th Ave, adjacent to the Poudre River Trail), a fenced 3-acre area with a covered shelter.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Greeley

The Poudre River Trail itself is Greeley's most walked/biked route, running 21 paved miles along the Cache la Poudre River. For a short in-town nature loop, Glenmere Park's roughly 0.8-mile paved trail circles a lake with abundant birdlife. For a true hike, many locals make the drive northeast to the Pawnee Buttes Trail in Pawnee National Grassland.

Schools & Commute From Greeley

Schools: Greeley-Evans School District 6. Commute: About 32 miles / 45-50 minutes to Fort Collins via US-34; about 57-63 miles / roughly 1 hour to Denver via I-25 or US-85.
