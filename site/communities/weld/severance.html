Welcome To Severance

Severance, Colorado, is a charming and rapidly growing town nestled in Northern Colorado. Known for its friendly community and scenic surroundings, Severance offers a perfect blend of small-town charm and modern development. With open spaces, great schools, and a relaxed pace of life, it’s an inviting place to settle and enjoy the beauty of Colorado living.

Things To Do In Severance

A local landmark first opened in 1957 (closed briefly in the mid-2000s and reopened under new ownership in 2008, carrying forward the original building and recipes), Bruce's Bar is renowned for its Rocky Mountain oysters and live music. Explore firefighting history at the Windsor-Severance Fire Museum (located just south in Windsor), which houses antique fire trucks and memorabilia. Located about six miles south in Windsor, Windsor Lake features a scenic 2.25-mile trail around its perimeter, a swim beach, and opportunities for fishing and boating. Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy the Great Western Trail, a 10.5-mile mixed-use path through Weld County. Note: Severance's Community Park closed in early 2026 for a construction/improvement phase expected to run through fall 2026, so Severance Days and the town's usual summer concert series have shifted to alternate venues and a later date for the year.

Restaurants & Dining

Bruce's Bar, a genuine Colorado landmark first opened in 1957 and reopened under new ownership in 2008, remains the town's marquee stop for Rocky Mountain oysters, buffalo and elk burgers, and live music. For casual Mexican food, Tecate Grill Severance is a local option, and Shebrews Coffee and Ice Cream rounds things out for a sweet treat.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Severance doesn't have its own dog park, but just down the road in Windsor, the Windsor Lake Trail & Dog Park offers a fenced off-leash area with direct lake access.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Severance

The Great Western Trail (10.5 miles, mixed-use, running through Windsor, Severance, and Eaton on a former rail bed) is the area's standout trail. The Windsor Lake Trail, a 2.25-mile paved path circling the lake in nearby Windsor, is an easy, scenic walking loop with its own dog park built in.

Schools & Commute From Severance

Schools: Weld RE-4 (Windsor). Commute: About 16 miles / 27 minutes to Fort Collins via CO-14. Severance is one of Weld County's fastest-growing communities, reflecting continued new-home construction in and around town.
