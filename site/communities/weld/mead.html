Mead

Welcome To Mead

Mead, Colorado, is a tranquil and friendly town that captures the essence of small-town living. Nestled between Longmont and Loveland, it offers beautiful open spaces, a strong sense of community, and easy access to Northern Colorado’s amenities. With its peaceful neighborhoods and scenic charm, Mead is a wonderful place to call home.

What It's Like To Live In Mead

Things To Do In Mead

Visit the historic Highlandlake Church, built in 1896 and placed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1989, which still stands and offers a glimpse into the area's past. Located about 7 miles south of Mead, St. Vrain State Park offers roughly 689 acres of land and 228 acres of water, making it a perfect place for camping, birdwatching, fishing, and hiking. The town of Mead also offers several public parks and walking trails for outdoor recreation and community gatherings.

Restaurants & Dining

Josie's Tacos on Main Street is a favorite for quick, authentic Mexican food, while Ranchero's Restaurant and Mead Pizza Plus, both on Hwy 66, serve up comfort food for locals. Coffee lovers should note Mead is home to the headquarters of Ziggi's Coffee, the regional drive-thru coffee chain.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Mead doesn't have its own dedicated off-leash dog park. The nearest real options are in Longmont, about 10-15 minutes away, including Rough and Ready Neighborhood Park and Blue Skies Neighborhood Park, both with fenced off-leash dog runs.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Mead

St. Vrain State Park, just south of town, has easy, flat walking trails around its ponds, ideal for casual walks and birdwatching. In town, the walking loop at Lorin Mead Park at Highland Lake offers a quieter, close-to-home option.

Schools & Commute From Mead

Schools: St. Vrain Valley School District RE-1J. Commute: About 13 miles / 18 minutes to Longmont via I-25; about 17 miles / 24 minutes to Loveland via I-25.
