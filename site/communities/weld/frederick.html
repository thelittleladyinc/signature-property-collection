Frederick

Welcome To Frederick

Frederick, Colorado, is a dynamic and growing town that combines small-town charm with modern living. Located in Weld County, it offers a welcoming community, scenic parks, and a rich history rooted in its mining heritage. With its convenient location near Denver and Boulder, Frederick provides easy access to big-city amenities while maintaining a peaceful, family-friendly atmosphere.

Life In Frederick

What It's Like To Live In Frederick

Things To Do In Frederick

Explore Frederick's Art in Public Places program, featuring public art installations and murals throughout town. Visit the Frederick Recreation Area, a 129-acre park offering a playground, picnic areas, a fishing pond, and paved walking trails around Milavec Reservoir. Learn about Frederick's mining heritage at the Miners Memorial Museum in Crist Park, open on a limited schedule (by request and select town events). Golf enthusiasts can tee off at the Bella Rosa Golf Course, a nine-hole course for players of all skill levels.

Restaurants & Dining

Mirror Image Brewing Company is a local brewery and brewpub, Rumbo 52 Cocina & Cantina covers Mexican fare, and Georgia Boys BBQ Smokehouse serves up barbecue.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Frederick has its own dog park on-site at the Frederick Recreation Area.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Frederick

The Frederick Recreation Area has 1.26 miles of crusher-fine trail around Milavec Reservoir plus 1.7 miles of paved trail, right in town. The St. Vrain Greenway Trail through nearby Longmont adds a longer paved option.

Schools & Commute From Frederick

Schools: St. Vrain Valley Schools (feeds into Frederick High School). Commute: About 28 miles / 28-37 minutes to Denver and roughly 22 miles / 34 minutes to Boulder — a fairly balanced commute to both metro job centers. Frederick is one of the fastest-growing towns in the region, up roughly 30% since the 2020 census, fueled by Denver/Boulder-area buyers seeking affordability.
