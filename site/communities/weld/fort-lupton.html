Fort Lupton

Welcome To Fort Lupton

Fort Lupton, Colorado, is a historic and welcoming town nestled along the South Platte River. Known for its rich heritage and small-town charm, it offers a peaceful lifestyle with convenient access to Denver and surrounding areas. With its close-knit community, open spaces, and ties to Colorado’s agricultural roots, Fort Lupton is a delightful place to call home.

What It's Like To Live In Fort Lupton

Things To Do In Fort Lupton

Explore the South Platte Valley Historic Park, which features a reconstructed adobe trading post built in 2009 on the site of the original 1830s-40s Fort Lupton, along with other historical buildings. Enjoy a round of golf at the Coyote Creek Golf Course, an 18-hole course suitable for players of all skill levels. Visit the Fort Lupton Museum (open Wednesday-Friday, 9am-3pm) to delve deeper into the city's history. About 20-25 minutes away, Barr Lake State Park offers hiking, bird-watching, and fishing along an 8.8-mile trail that circles the lake.

Restaurants & Dining

Wholly Stromboli, a beloved New York-style pizzeria and bar downtown, is often cited as the town's standout eatery, and Santiago's III Mexican Restaurant is a go-to for breakfast burritos.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Fort Lupton doesn't currently have a dedicated off-leash dog park in town — Pearson Park and Railroad Park are dog-friendly on leash, with walking paths along the South Platte River. For a true off-leash run, the nearest option is Happy Tails Dog Park in Brighton, roughly a 15-minute drive.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Fort Lupton

The Barr Lake Lake Loop / Perimeter Trail (8.8 miles, flat and multi-use) is one of the best easy hikes in the region, about a 20-minute drive from town. Closer to home, Pearson Park's trails wind along the South Platte River through wetlands and open meadows.

Schools & Commute From Fort Lupton

Schools: Weld RE-8 School District, headquartered in Fort Lupton. Commute: About 29 miles / 43 minutes to Denver via US-85 — a direct north-south commute route into the metro area without needing I-25. Fort Lupton has grown more modestly than its neighboring I-25-corridor towns, which may appeal to buyers wanting a quieter, more historic South Platte River town within commuting range of Denver.
