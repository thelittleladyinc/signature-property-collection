Dacono

Welcome To Dacono

Dacono, Colorado, is a charming and close-knit community offering a relaxed, small-town vibe. Located just off I-25, it provides convenient access to Denver, Boulder, and Northern Colorado. With its scenic surroundings, friendly atmosphere, and growing amenities, Dacono is an inviting place to call home.

Life In Dacono

What It's Like To Live In Dacono

Things To Do In Dacono

Experience the thrill of live auto racing at the Colorado National Speedway, a premier NASCAR-sanctioned track offering exciting events throughout the racing season. Enjoy outdoor activities at Clem Dufour Park, featuring a splash pad, playgrounds, sports courts, and picnic areas, making it a perfect spot for family outings. Join the community at the annual Dacono Music & Spirits Festival, a one-day event featuring live music, local distilleries, food vendors, and family-friendly activities. For adventure seekers, Blitz Paintball offers an expansive facility with various fields and game scenarios suitable for all skill levels. Test your skills at the IMI Motorsports Complex, which provides kart racing, motocross tracks, and other motorsport activities for enthusiasts.

Restaurants & Dining

Dacono is a small town, so dining options are modest and mostly local Mexican spots. El Taco Loco II and Santiago's II Mexican Restaurant are both well-reviewed local favorites, and Wing Shack Dacono is the go-to for wings and casual bar food.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Dacono itself does not have a dedicated off-leash dog park. The nearest confirmed options are The Boneyard at Reliance Park in Erie or Broomfield County Commons Dog Park, both a 15-20 minute drive.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Dacono

The Firestone Trail, a mostly-paved path connecting Firestone, Frederick, and Dacono, extends into Dacono via its southern Old Railroad Trail segment. St. Vrain State Park, just north in Firestone, offers miles of interconnected, mostly ADA-accessible trails around several ponds.

Schools & Commute From Dacono

Schools: St. Vrain Valley School District RE-1J. Commute: About 28 miles / 39 minutes to Denver via I-25, with direct I-25 access into both Denver and Boulder. Dacono is one of the fastest-growing towns in southern Weld County — population rose over 51% from 2010 to 2020 — driven largely by Denver/Boulder-area buyers seeking more affordable housing.
