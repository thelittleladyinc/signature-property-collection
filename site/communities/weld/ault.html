Living In Ault, Colorado

Welcome To Ault

Ault is a small, close-knit agricultural town in Weld County, sitting along US Highway 85 just a few miles north of Eaton and about 12 miles north of Greeley. Founded in 1904, Ault has held onto its farming roots even as the broader Front Range grows up around it. It's the kind of place where Main Street antique shops, a slower rhythm, and open prairie views still define daily life, and where neighbors genuinely know each other. For buyers who want small-town quiet, easy highway access to Greeley and Fort Collins, and land that still feels like Colorado's high plains, Ault offers a slower pace without being far from anything.

Life In Ault

What It's Like To Live In Ault

Things To Do In Ault

Liberty Park is Ault's community park and the site of the town's annual Fall Festival each August. A small strip of local antique and secondhand shops along Main Street draws browsers from around Weld County. Ault also sits along the route east on CO-14 toward Pawnee National Grassland, home to the Pawnee Buttes Trail and some of the best birdwatching in Northern Colorado, roughly an hour's drive from town. Because Ault is very small, residents rely on nearby Eaton (about 6 miles south) for the A.J. Eaton Home and Carriage House Museum and the Eaton Area Community Center, and on Greeley (about 12 miles south) for larger shopping, dining, and entertainment.

Restaurants & Dining

Ault is small, but it does have a handful of genuine local spots, including a local cafe and pizza shop on Main Street. For a wider selection, Eaton (6 miles) and Greeley (12 miles) have considerably more dining options.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Ault does not have its own dog park. The nearest real off-leash options are Rover Run Dog Park in Greeley (about 12 miles) or Poudre Pooch Park in Windsor (about 16 miles). Liberty Park in Ault is a fine leashed-dog spot for a local walk.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Ault

The Pawnee Buttes Trail in Pawnee National Grassland is a roughly 2-mile trail to the base of the first Pawnee Butte, with unique rock formations, reached via CO-14 east of Ault. The Great Western Trail, about a 10-15 minute drive south, and the Poudre River Trail, about 15-20 minutes south, are the closest longer trails.

Downtown Ault

I grew up around here. There's more to Ault than the name most buyers read on a highway sign at 60mph — a couple of good places to eat and a small-town character that's getting harder to find.

Where To Eat

Gray's Cafe

One of our favourite places to eat, right on 1st Street downtown. Breakfast only runs until 2:00, so don't turn up at half past expecting eggs.

Winery

BBC Wines

Teresa and her husband make the wine here themselves, in what used to be a tractor store — she moved the whole operation up from Eaton to do it. Go on a reservation rather than turning up; it isn't a walk-in kind of place.
