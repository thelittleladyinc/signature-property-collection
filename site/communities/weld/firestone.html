Firestone

Welcome To Firestone

Firestone, Colorado, is a vibrant and family-friendly town known for its stunning mountain views and welcoming community. Conveniently located near Longmont and Denver, it offers a perfect mix of small-town charm and modern amenities. With its numerous parks, trails, and recreational opportunities, Firestone provides a high quality of life in the heart of Northern Colorado.

Life In Firestone

What It's Like To Live In Firestone

Things To Do In Firestone

Enjoy outdoor activities at St. Vrain State Park, which offers opportunities for fishing, camping, and hiking amidst scenic landscapes. Visit Wild Cider to sample a variety of locally crafted ciders in a welcoming atmosphere. Golf enthusiasts can enjoy a round at the Saddleback Golf Club, known for its well-maintained course and beautiful views. Explore a wide selection of books and community programs at the Carbon Valley Regional Library, a great resource for all ages. Relax and play at Neighbors Point Park, a local park offering open spaces and recreational facilities for families and individuals.

Restaurants & Dining

Pepper's Fireside Grille is a local American grill, Wild Cider doubles as a dining destination alongside its cidery, and Maya Grill & Cantina covers Mexican fare.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Firestone's two former dog parks (Settlers Park and Aisik's Meadow) have closed. As of this writing there is no confirmed operating off-leash dog park within Firestone town limits — the nearest options are The Boneyard at Reliance Park in Erie or dog-friendly trails around Longmont, and St. Vrain State Park allows leashed pets on its trails.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Firestone

St. Vrain State Park's roughly 7 miles of trails wind around Blue Heron Reservoir and the park's ponds. The St. Vrain Greenway Trail, a 17.5-mile paved path through nearby Longmont, is about a 15-minute drive away.

Schools & Commute From Firestone

Schools: St. Vrain Valley Schools (includes Firestone Charter Academy, feeds into Frederick High School). Commute: About 30 miles / 41 minutes to Denver via I-25, and roughly 12-15 minutes to Longmont — dual access to Denver and Boulder-area jobs. Firestone is part of the strongest growth pocket in Weld County (alongside Frederick, Dacono, and Erie), driven by relative affordability compared to Boulder County and metro Denver.
