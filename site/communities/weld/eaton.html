Eaton

Welcome To Eaton

Eaton, Colorado, is a welcoming agricultural town with a rich history and a strong sense of community. Located just north of Greeley, it offers a peaceful, small-town lifestyle surrounded by Colorado’s scenic beauty. Known for its excellent schools and family-friendly atmosphere, Eaton is an ideal place to enjoy a quiet and fulfilling way of life.

What It's Like To Live In Eaton

Things To Do In Eaton

Delve into Eaton's history by visiting the A.J. Eaton Home and Carriage House Museum. Opened in 2017, the Eaton Area Community Center offers a fitness center, indoor track, and leisure pool. Outdoor enthusiasts can explore the Great Western Trail, a 10.5-mile unpaved rail-trail running through Eaton, Severance, and Windsor, ideal for hiking, biking, and horseback riding. Experience local culture at the annual Eaton Days Festival. Discover a unique collection of antique washing machines at the Lee Maxwell Washing Machine Museum, showcasing over 1,800 machines — the world's largest collection of its kind.

Restaurants & Dining

Casa Maria Mexican Kitchen & Margaritas is a family-owned favorite for fajitas and margaritas. Route 85 Grill serves casual American fare including weekend breakfast, and McCarty's Restaurant at Eaton Country Club is open to the public for burgers, steaks, and patio dining.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Eaton itself has no dedicated off-leash dog park. The nearest real option is Rover Run Dog Park in Greeley (about 10 miles) or Poudre Pooch Park in Windsor (about 12 miles). Locally, Eaton Commons Park has walking paths suitable for leashed dogs.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Eaton

The Great Western Trail runs 10.5 miles unpaved through Eaton, Severance, and Windsor. The Poudre River Trail, a 21-mile paved path, runs from Greeley to Windsor about 10 miles south, and the Pawnee Buttes Trail in Pawnee National Grassland is a genuine regional draw about an hour's drive northeast.

Schools & Commute From Eaton

Schools: Weld RE-2 School District (Eaton School District). Commute: About 9 miles / roughly 15-17 minutes to Greeley via US-85, Eaton's primary job center. Eaton hosts Eaton Days, its signature annual community festival — a good small-town-camaraderie selling point for relocation buyers.
