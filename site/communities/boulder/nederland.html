Welcome To Nederland

Nederland, Colorado, is a quaint mountain town known for its rich history, artistic spirit, and stunning natural surroundings. Located in Boulder County near the Continental Divide, it offers a peaceful, laid-back lifestyle with access to endless outdoor adventures. With its unique charm and strong sense of community, Nederland is a perfect retreat for those seeking a connection to nature and a welcoming mountain vibe.

Life In Nederland

What It's Like To Live In Nederland

Things To Do In Nederland

Experience the Carousel of Happiness, a hand-carved, restored 1910 Looff carousel featuring 56 whimsical animals, accompanied by the music of a 1913 Wurlitzer band organ. Eldora Mountain Resort offers skiing and snowboarding in winter, with the surrounding Indian Peaks Wilderness open for summer hiking. Explore the outdoors via the Hessie Trailhead, a gateway to numerous hiking trails leading to alpine lakes. Delve into the town's rich mining history at the Nederland Mining Museum, open seasonally June through October. Relax by Barker Reservoir, a scenic spot ideal for picnics, fishing, and mountain views.

Restaurants & Dining

Crosscut Pizzeria & Taphouse is well-loved for wood-fired pizza and a mountain-town patio. Kathmandu Restaurant brings genuine Nepali and Indian cuisine to a town better known for burgers than curry, and Salto Coffee Works is the local go-to for espresso before a day on the trails.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Nederland itself does not have a dedicated fenced dog park. Dogs are welcome off-leash under voice-and-sight control on many surrounding Boulder County and U.S. Forest Service trails near town. For a true fenced off-leash dog park, the nearest options are down the canyon in Boulder, about 17 miles (roughly a 30-35 minute drive).

Best Hikes & Trails Near Nederland

From the Hessie Trailhead, the hike to Lost Lake is a short, scenic favorite with wildflowers and mountain views. Diamond Lake and Devil's Thumb Lake are popular longer hikes into the Indian Peaks Wilderness, and the trail around Barker Reservoir offers an easy, flat walk right at Nederland's edge.

Schools & Commute From Nederland

Schools: Boulder Valley School District (BVSD, Re-2) — served locally by Nederland Elementary and Nederland Middle-Senior High School. Commute: About 49 miles / roughly 1 hour 20 minutes to Loveland via CO-119/US-36; about 17 miles / roughly 32 minutes to Boulder. Nederland's town board approved a roughly $120M purchase of Eldora Mountain Resort from POWDR Corporation (approved January 2026) — if finalized, Eldora would become a rare community/municipally-owned ski resort, a distinctive story for buyers drawn to mountain-town character.
