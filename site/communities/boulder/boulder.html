Welcome To Boulder

Boulder, Colorado, is a vibrant city renowned for its stunning natural beauty, progressive culture, and outdoor lifestyle. Nestled at the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, Boulder offers endless recreational opportunities, a thriving arts scene, and a commitment to sustainability. With its dynamic community, world-class dining, and picturesque surroundings, Boulder is a truly unique place to live and explore.

Life In Boulder

What It's Like To Live In Boulder

Things To Do In Boulder

Explore the Pearl Street Mall, a vibrant pedestrian area featuring a mix of local shops, restaurants, and street performers. It's an ideal spot for shopping, dining, and soaking in the local culture. Hike the iconic Flatirons, the distinctive rock formations that are a symbol of Boulder. Numerous trails cater to various skill levels, offering breathtaking views of the city and surrounding areas. Visit Chautauqua Park, a historic area that provides access to hiking trails, cultural events, and the Chautauqua Dining Hall. It's a perfect place to experience both nature and Boulder's community events. Experience local produce and artisan goods at the Boulder Farmers Market, held weekly and offering a variety of fresh foods, crafts, and live music. It's a great way to engage with the local community and enjoy fresh, local products. Boulder Museum of Contemporary Art (BMoCA) Explore contemporary art exhibits at the Boulder Museum of Contemporary Art, which showcases innovative artworks and hosts various cultural events throughout the year.

Restaurants & Dining

Frasca Food and Wine is a nationally recognized spot for refined Northern Italian cuisine and an exceptional wine list. For a splurge with a view, The Flagstaff House perches on Flagstaff Mountain with panoramic city views. Locals swear by The Sink, a beloved century-old diner near CU Boulder famous for its burgers, and Illegal Pete's is a go-to for burritos after a hike.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Boulder is highly dog-friendly, with several designated off-leash areas managed by the city, including the Foothills Community Park Dog Park and the Howard Heuston Off-Leash Dog Area. Boulder also allows leash-free hiking with a Voice & Sight tag on many Open Space and Mountain Parks (OSMP) trails.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Boulder

The Chautauqua Trail up to the First and Second Flatirons Loop is the classic Boulder hike, climbing right from Chautauqua Park into the iconic red-rock formations. The Royal Arch Trail, also starting at Chautauqua, is a steeper climb rewarded by a natural rock arch, and Mount Sanitas near downtown is a favorite steep loop for locals.

Schools & Commute From Boulder

Schools: Boulder Valley School District (BVSD, Re-2). Commute: About 33 miles / roughly 48 minutes to Loveland via US-36 and CO-119. Boulder's market is tight and ultra-premium: median single-family price runs roughly $1.05M-$1.15M with limited active inventory, and luxury demand concentrates in neighborhoods like Mapleton Hill and Wonderland Hills — partly a result of Boulder's building-height limits and city-owned open space greenbelt constraining new supply.
